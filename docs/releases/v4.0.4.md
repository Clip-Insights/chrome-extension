# Clip Insights 4.0.4

Account settings and usage information inside the video study panel.

- Open Settings to see account profile details and the effective plan.
- View summary and chat usage with remaining allowances and reset timing.
- Manage the session through the Settings view.
- Use backend-supplied plan values for the note, screenshot, and AI limits shown by the extension.

The workspace includes timestamped screenshots and notes, AI summaries and key points, streamed chat, transcript copy, and PDF export. Notes and screenshots are stored locally; uploaded PDFs appear in the web dashboard.
