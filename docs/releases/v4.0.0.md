# Clip Insights 4.0.0

A YouTube study workspace with timestamped notes, screenshots, AI insights, and PDF export.

## Study tools

- Capture a video frame and attach it to the playback timestamp.
- Write notes alongside the video and return to a moment from its timestamp.
- Generate a summary and key points from the video transcript.
- Ask questions in a streamed conversation about the video.
- Copy the transcript and export study notes as a PDF.
- Sign in with email/password or Google to use account features.

## Application structure

The extension uses React, TypeScript, Vite, and Manifest V3. The study panel runs inside a Shadow DOM. A YouTube platform adapter supplies video context, transcript access, and page navigation. Working notes and screenshots use IndexedDB; account and AI features use the Clip Insights backend.

AI features require sign-in, available video captions, an internet connection, and allowance under the account plan. Local working records have a five-day retention period; export a PDF to keep a session.
